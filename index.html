Install Say Halo

  1. Download the app with the button above. Double-click the ZIP in Finder to open it.
  2. Drag Say Halo.app into Applications, then open it there.
  3. Setup opens on your first launch. Follow each step in the app.

Already installed? Quit Say Halo before replacing it. Your settings and downloaded speech models stay in place.

Try your first dictation

Setup helps you download a speech model, test your microphone and choose a shortcut. The model needs an internet connection to download.

  • Microphone lets Say Halo hear you.
  • Accessibility is needed for the shortcut, automatic text insertion and Finish Setup.
  • Input Monitoring is offered only if the shortcut test cannot hear your keys.

After allowing Accessibility, click inside a text box in another app and try the shortcut shown in Setup.

Want to try recording first? In Setup, open First dictation (optional) and choose Record practice. Stop the recording and wait for the text. Open Troubleshooting, choose Copy transcript, then paste it into another app with Command-V. This works without Accessibility, but does not finish setup or test the shortcut.

To return to Setup, click Say Halo's waveform icon in the menu bar, choose Settings, then Setup.

Your voice and text

Speech-to-text runs on your Mac. By default, History saves the text and audio of your dictations on your Mac. You can turn History off in Settings.

Inserting text uses your clipboard. The app you paste into handles that text under its own privacy rules.

Speech model downloads and app updates use the internet. This page has no analytics, cookies, forms or third-party scripts.

If Say Halo will not open

If macOS says it cannot verify the app is free of malware, stop and report the message, your macOS version and your Say Halo version to the person who gave you the app. Keep the download for diagnosis. Leave macOS security turned on.

Installed version 0.8.12 or earlier? Download the current app once. Later versions use Say Halo's automatic updater.
