# Say Halo 0.8.28

- Fixes the microphone getting stuck after screen sharing or switching audio devices. Say Halo no longer sits at "Warming up…" or "Mic stopped" until you quit and reopen it.
- If the microphone stops responding, Say Halo replaces it with a fresh connection within a few seconds. Reconnect works again instead of doing nothing.
- A microphone that looks on but sends no sound is now detected and rebuilt before your next dictation.
- As a last resort, Say Halo quietly restarts itself in the menu bar, after any dictation in progress finishes.

Existing settings, saved word replacements, models, and permissions are preserved.
