# Say Halo 0.8.27

- A compact menu puts recording controls and microphone selection in one row. Start or stop with the circular control and choose an input directly from the menu.
- See your last transcript and when it was created. Hover to preview more, then click anywhere on the row to copy the full text.
- Save word replacements directly in the menu with From → To and the plus button, or press Enter. The saved count and Manage link sit beside Quick replace.
- Microphone-health checks can retry a stopped recording connection once and show Reconnect if capture remains unavailable. Quiet pauses are treated as normal.
- Downloaded updates appear as a clear install-and-restart row. Settings and Quit keep their standard keyboard shortcuts.
- Cleaner text, a bright copy icon, a solid menu background, compact microphone options, and a right-aligned device name improve readability.

Existing settings, saved word replacements, models, and permissions are preserved.
