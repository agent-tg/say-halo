# Say Halo 0.8.17

**Watch setup tutorial** now opens the new, clearer version of the one-minute Setup video. It walks through permissions, the speech model, microphone and shortcut checks, a practice sentence, and what to do if text doesn't appear in another app.

[Watch the setup tutorial](https://youtu.be/9M0w5RP3Hko). Open Setup from Settings or **Setup & Troubleshooting…** in the menu bar.

Download the ZIP under Assets, extract it, and move **Say Halo.app** into **Applications**. Requires macOS 14 or later. Existing settings are retained.
