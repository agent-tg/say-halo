## Say Halo 0.8.43

- A dictation is no longer lost when saving it to Transcripts fails: the text is pasted first.
- If the microphone reconnects mid-recording, the words from before the reconnect are kept.
- A stuck transcription now stops with a message and keeps the recording. The menu bar says Transcribing instead of Warming up.
- Two quick dictations in a row put back your own clipboard, not the first dictation.
- One bad setting no longer resets the rest, and your Replace rules are kept.
- Copy last transcript works when saving transcripts is off.
