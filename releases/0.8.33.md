## Say Halo 0.8.33

- The first time you open Say Halo, Setup walks you through one step at a time.
- Input Monitoring is no longer required.
- Until Accessibility is turned on, your dictation is copied to the clipboard and a "Copied" notice tells you so. Paste it wherever you need it.
- Settings now has four pages: General, History, Replace and Setup.
- The Replace list shows your newest replacements first, and its controls stay in reach while you scroll.
- Shortcuts show key names, like "⌥ Option", instead of symbols alone.
