# Say Halo 0.8.15

Setup is more compact, with shorter instructions and less scrolling. Find it below About in the settings sidebar, or choose **Setup & Troubleshooting…** from the menu bar.

- Completed steps collapse and can reopen. Help appears when requested or when a step fails.
- Record a practice sentence, test dictation in another app, and confirm whether the text appeared. Copy and troubleshooting are available if insertion fails.
- Clipboard guidance follows your current settings. Existing microphone, shortcut, model, and clipboard preferences stay intact.

Download the ZIP under Assets, extract it, and move **Say Halo.app** into **Applications**. Requires macOS 14 or later.
