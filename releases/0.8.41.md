## Say Halo 0.8.41

- The sidebar now reads Settings, Replace, Transcripts, with Onboarding at the bottom.
- Settings shows your version and Check for Updates at the top. The Advanced section is gone; each setting now sits with the section it belongs to.
- Shortcuts show as key caps with one Change button.
- Transcripts: search moved to the left, and Delete all moved into Settings.
- Onboarding has a new step that helps you find Say Halo in your menu bar.
- Added an Acknowledgements page in Settings.
