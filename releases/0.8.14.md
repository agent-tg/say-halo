# Say Halo 0.8.14

Say Halo now guides new users from first launch through their first dictation and paste. Open **Setup & Troubleshooting…** from the menu bar whenever you need help.

- Check permissions, choose and prepare a speech model, and test your active microphone.
- Test your current shortcut and choose clipboard insertion, without changing existing preferences.
- Practice dictation, then test pasting in another app. Copy the result manually if the destination does not accept it.
- Model downloads now show measured component progress where available and a separate preparation state. Retry after errors without deleting existing models.
- Disconnected microphone choices clearly show the fallback device and a recovery action.

Download the ZIP under Assets, extract it, and move **Say Halo.app** into **Applications**. Requires macOS 14 or later. Existing users keep their settings and can revisit Setup from the menu bar.
