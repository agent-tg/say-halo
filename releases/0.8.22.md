Say Halo 0.8.22 improves isolation of left and right modifier shortcuts.

- A shortcut assigned to Right or Left now requires a matching known modifier side. Events without side information no longer start or stop that side-specific shortcut.
- Either continues to accept both sides. To keep Left Command paste and screenshot shortcuts separate from dictation, choose Right under Settings → Hot Key → Modifier side.
- To keep the recording timer visible in full-screen apps, set macOS Automatically hide and show the menu bar to Never.

This update preserves existing shortcut settings. It does not change Either to Right automatically.
