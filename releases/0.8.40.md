## Say Halo 0.8.40

- The settings window is simpler. The four pages are now Settings, Transcripts, Replace and Onboarding.
- Settings shows everyday controls first. Fine-tuning is under Advanced.
- Transcripts has Today, 7 days, 30 days and All, plus search.
- Replace has search, sorting and examples for each formatting switch.
- Onboarding shows every step without opening anything. Once you finish, it becomes a short health check.
