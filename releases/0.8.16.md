# Say Halo 0.8.16

This update adds **Watch setup tutorial** to the compact Setup screen. The one-minute video walks through permissions, model loading, microphone and shortcut checks, a practice transcription, and the manual Copy fallback.

[Watch the setup tutorial](https://youtu.be/RGCBG8OjeGM). Open Setup from Settings or **Setup & Troubleshooting…** in the menu bar.

Download the ZIP under Assets, extract it, and move **Say Halo.app** into **Applications**. Requires macOS 14 or later. Existing settings are retained.
