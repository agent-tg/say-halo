## Say Halo 0.8.44

- Pressing your shortcut while the last dictation is still turning into text starts a new one. The first one still pastes.
- Escape cancels only while you are recording. Once you stop, your words always arrive.
- Quit and Update wait for a dictation in progress to finish and paste first.
- Turning off Save transcripts asks before deleting the ones you have, and you can keep them. Lowering how many to keep asks before deleting old ones.
