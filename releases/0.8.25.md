# Say Halo 0.8.25

- Choose **Add Word Replacement…** from the menu bar, enter **Heard** and **Replace with**, and save your preferred spelling for future dictation.
- Assign an optional global shortcut in **Settings → Word Replacements** to open quick entry from any app.
- Saving an existing match updates and enables its saved replacement.

Your existing settings, replacements, and iCloud sync choice are preserved.
